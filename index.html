THE STORY BEHIND THE STORY

A myth. A different navigator.

This independent student prototype retells three episodes from Homer's Odyssey: Polyphemus (Book 9), Circe (Book 10), and the Sirens (Book 12). Conceived with Christopher Nolan’s adaptation in mind, its branching dialogue, consequences and endings are original creative interpretations, not a reconstruction of the film.

In the poem, Odysseus blinds the Cyclops and escapes beneath sheep, confronts Circe with Hermes’ protection, and hears the Sirens while bound to the mast. Here, you can choose other paths. Some choices interrupt the voyage and invite you to retry; successful paths continue towards Ithaca.

All illustrations are original vector artwork. Optional ambient audio is synthesised in your browser. No film clips, stills or soundtrack are used.

Primary text: Book 9 · Book 10 · Book 12, A. T. Murray translation (1919), Theoi Classical Texts Library.
